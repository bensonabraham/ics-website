About the Archive

PRESERVING OUR SHARED HISTORY

ICSKWT Archives is an independent, non-commercial initiative dedicated to preserving photographs, videos and other media related to Indian Central School, Kuwait. Much of this material exists across old social-media posts, personal devices and other sources, where it can gradually become lost, inaccessible or permanently disappear over time.

The project focuses on collecting and preserving school memories, particularly pre-pandemic media, so that photographs and footage documenting the experiences of past generations are not forgotten. It exists solely for preservation, awareness and accessibility, providing the ICS community with a place to discover, revisit and help preserve its shared history.

Collection Highlights
  • Photo Archives Extensive archive of images from 1990s to late 2020s, covering events in both the old as well as the new campus blocks.
  • ICSKWT Archives PlaylistDedicated YouTube Playlist with footages from public sources exploring the campus and its unique vibes.
  • Instagram Outreach ProgrammeDesigned to expand the project's reach among the alumni community, both within Kuwait and internationally.
  • Physical Memorabilia Collection of screenshots, documents and other media giving a vivid picture of the school life those days
Timeline

A Historical Perspective

Trace the defining eras of Indian Central School. This timeline highlights key periods represented within our current archival holdings.

  • 1994

    The Beginning

    Founded by Mr. Thomas Chandy with founding principal Mrs. Asha Sharma, the school began functioning on 31 August 1994, initially serving students up to Class VIII. The school recorded about 200 students when it came into existence.

  • 1995

    The First Expansion

    Class IX was added in April 1995. The school also became a member of the Council of CBSE Affiliated Schools in the Gulf.

  • 1996

    CBSE Affiliation

    Indian Central School became officially affiliated to CBSE in 1996 and was subsequently upgraded.

  • 1997

    First Class X Board Batch

    The first batch of Class X students appeared for the CBSE Board Examination in March 1997, defining a milestone by achieving a 100% result, with 99.4% securing first class.

  • 1998

    100% Results Continue

    From the second Class X batch in March 1998, the school established its tradition of achieving 100% results year after year.

  • 1999

    Senior Secondary Education Begins

    The transition to a complete senior-secondary institution arrived as the first batch of Class XII students appeared for the CBSE Board Examination with Science and Commerce streams.

  • 2000s

    A Decade of Growth

    An era of expansion saw the school rapidly develop its academic programmes, infrastructure, and an increasing body of student activities.

  • 2006

    End of the Founding Principal Era

    Mrs. Asha Sharma departed after 12 years of foundational leadership at Indian Central School.

  • 2007

    Dr. Shanta Maria James Becomes Principal

    Dr. Shanta Maria James stepped into the role of Principal, inaugurating a new period of institutional growth.

  • 2014

    Twenty Years of ICS

    The school marked its 20th anniversary celebrations, having grown significantly since its inception to serve an expanding student base.

  • 2014–2016

    Academic Distinction

    ICS achieved top rankings in Kuwait in streams such as Biotechnology and Humanities, solidifying its academic reputation.

  • 2016

    Major CBSE Sports Achievements & Science Prominence

    The school continued its strong showing in CBSE cluster events and further elevated its presence in regional science exhibitions.

  • 2017

    New Campus

    Indian Central School moved into a new state-of-the-art building with modern facilities, including smart classrooms, laboratories, indoor sports facilities, swimming pools and other infrastructure.

  • 2017

    A Long Sporting Tradition

    The school celebrated its 16th consecutive CBSE Athletics Championship victory among Kuwait schools.

  • 2018

    Gyanutsav and Innovation

    Major initiatives such as Gyanutsav highlighted the school's commitment to innovation and extensive co-curricular development.

  • 2019

    A New Management Era

    Dar Al-Salam Educational Group took over the management of Indian Central School, beginning a new chapter of administration.

  • 2020

    The Pandemic

    The COVID-19 pandemic necessitated a shift to digital learning, marking the boundary between pre-pandemic archival media and the digital-native activities that followed.

  • 2021

    New Principal

    Mrs. Sujata Sivakrishnan assumed the role of Principal to guide the school through the post-pandemic educational landscape.

  • 2022

    New Educational Initiatives

    The introduction of NIOS and Robotics programs added diverse pathways and modern technological education for the students.

  • 2023

    Continued Growth & Alumni Connections

    The school expanded its reach with growing enrollment and strengthened ties with its international alumni network.

  • 2024

    Three Decades of ICS

    Indian Central School celebrated its 30-year milestone, standing as a large established institution with more than 5,300 students.

  • 2025–Present

    The Continuing Legacy

    The school continues to develop its academic, sporting, and co-curricular programmes under the stewardship of its faculty and management.

Major Themes

Scale of Growth

From an initial ~200 students in 1994, the school has expanded to serve over 5,300 students, requiring the move to a larger, state-of-the-art campus in 2017, a move which is frowned upon by many people even today.

Leadership Transitions

Guided by founding principal Mrs. Asha Sharma, followed by Dr. Shanta Maria James and currently Mrs. Sujata Sivakrishnan, alongside the 2019 management transition to Dar Al-Salam Educational Group.
